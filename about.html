Hi, I'm

Melissa Hundley

Junior Frontend Developer, Creative Professional, and Business Owner

Based in Damascus, Oregon, USA

I am a junior developer and creative entrepreneur. I have seven years experience working in technology services (six of those at a local university). In that time, I primarily worked in IT project and program management but took the opportunities to learn other skills from the team.

My favorite tools to work with are my trusty Mac, VS Code, GitHub, my iPad Pro and Apple Pencil, Procreate, and Canva.

My journey with calligraphy began in junior high when I took my very first lettering course. What started as a simple art elective became a passion as an adult. Over the years, I’ve continued to be a student of calligraphy, always excited to learn new styles, techniques, and ways to grow as an artist.

Almost all of my designs begin by hand - whether with pen and paper, or on my iPad in Procreate. I find calligraphy and hand-lettering to be a wonderful way to slow myself down and focus on a single thing. I believe what we surround ourselves with matters and because of that, I try to surround myself with scripture, and visual reminders of my faith and identity.

My hope is that my designs and services inspire, uplift, and encourage people in their everyday lives - whether that is through something you wear, something on your wall, or an app you use everyday.

When I'm not working on coding projects, lettering or drawing something, or learning new skills, I enjoy hanging out with friends, playing cozy video games, music (I play several instruments), and spending time at the beach.
