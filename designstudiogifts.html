MJH Design Services Gifts

Online Etsy Shop



One of my favorite things to do is to create a custom, hand-lettered piece of art for a friend or family member.

So often, friends and family that have been the recipient of such gifts have told me I should open an Etsy shop to sell my artwork...so I finally did!

MJH Design Studio Gifts is my Etsy shop where I sell clothing and household items that I have designed myself and have a print provider do the actual printing and shipping.

The images above are links to the items in my shop. My designs are almost always inspired by scripture or my own personal faith journey and my prayer is that they will be a blessing to all who receive any item either for themselves, or as a gift.


I also love to assist with any bulk orders you may need - either for a birthday part, girls weekend away, or other event. If you are looking for something special and don't see what you need in my shop, let me know! I'm happy to work with you to create a design and can often offer a discount for bulk orders!
