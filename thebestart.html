The Best Art I've Ever Arted

Stepping Outside of my Comfort Zone to Create a Special Gift

8/19/2026 by Melissa Hundley


I know pretty much nothing about Hebrew calligraphy, or the language...except that it's read right to left. So someone tell me why I thought it was a good...no great idea to create a piece of calligraphy of Old Testament scripture that included Hebrew calligraphy?!?!?!

Just over a year ago, my job, and the job most of the staff where I worked lost our jobs. Reasons were given and were what they were, but it was really hard. I had come to love our little community. I loved that we got to serve students; teach them, mentor them, and care for them. I worked in IT Services and my position like so many others was cut. With basically four months notice, myself and so many others wanted to make sure we ended well for our students and for each other.


We celebrated a lot in that final semester. We celebrated our legacy, our students, and each other. There were so many people that had made my time there extra special and I wanted to find a way to let them know that I saw them and was so grateful to them...so I did what I knew I could do. I created artwork for many of them. There were so many I loved (including the "Be Still" piece above)...but one stood out more than the others...it was "the best art I've ever arted in my life."


I know I shouldn't have favorite coworkers...but let's be honest...I kinda did...(although I did have a lot of favorites...). One of my favorites was one of our Bible/Theology professors. Absolutely brilliant, and not very tech-savvy - which is where our friendship seemed to blossom. She would call or email me directly when she ran into an issue becuase she knew I'd figure it out and explain it in a way she could understand. Anyway...I knew I wanted to create something extra special for her.

I knew she loved the Old Testament, and she loved Hebrew...so I did what any crazy artist would do...I decided I was going to write a passage in Hewbrew calligraphy and give it to her (insert facepalm here). Since I knew almost nothing about Hebrew, I found one of her students and asked if he would help me find an accurate translation with the actual Hebrew letter forms. He absolutely delivered, and I went back to my studio and started figuring out what I could.

This whole process terrified me, and I was so nervous to give it to her because I was afraid I had runied such a beautiful passage of scripture without knowing it. I gave it to her at graduation and she LOVED it! I was so happy and relieved and so blessed! She even texted me to tell me she has it displayed very prominently in her podcast studio where she can see it every day!


I'm absolutely posting the picture again because I am proud of it. It still is "the best art I've ever arted" and I love the personal touch of being able to create it for someone who will appreciate the small details. If you have someone you want to give a special, customized gift to, let me know! Let's work together to create something meaningful and special!
