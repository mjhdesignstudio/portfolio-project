Welcome to


MJH Design Studio Gifts!

Faith-inspired art for everyday life

I’m a calligrapher and artist with a deep love for meaningful words, beautiful lettering, and creating pieces that encourage the heart. Much of my inspiration comes from my faith journey and from Scripture, which is at the center of both my life and my creative process.

My journey with calligraphy began in junior high when I took my very first lettering course. What started as a simple art elective became a passion as an adult. Over the years, I’ve continued to be a student of calligraphy, always excited to learn new styles, techniques, and ways to grow as an artist.

Nearly every design in this shop begins by hand—whether with ink on paper or digitally on my iPad in Procreate. I personally hand-letter most of the words you see and illustrate many of the artistic elements that accompany them. Each design is created thoughtfully, with the hope that it carries more than just visual beauty.

I created this shop because I believe what we surround ourselves with matters. The words we wear, display, and carry with us can shape our thoughts, strengthen our faith, and remind us of truth when we need it most.

My hope is that my designs inspire, uplift, and encourage you in your everyday life—whether through something you wear, gift to someone you love, or keep in your home as a daily reminder of hope and truth.
