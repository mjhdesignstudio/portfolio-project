Web App Development Services

Let me help you bring your web app ideas to life!



One of the coolest things I have learned in my life is how to take an idea for an app and bring it to life!

While I'm still learning some of the finer points of back-end development, I am able to create fully functional, well-designed apps to the web. I work primarily with React.js, as well as vanilla HTML and JavaScript depending on the scope of the application.

My coding training was done primarily through the SheCodes bootcamp, a coding bootcamp for wormen who want to learn coding skills. Through this bootcamp, I learned HTML, JavaScript, CSS, Python, React.js, and Responsive web development with CSS and Boostrap.
