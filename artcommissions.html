Commission Services

Customized calligraphy artwork offerings


One of my favorite things to do is to create a custom, hand-lettered piece of art for a friend or family member.

These have become my go-to gifts for baby showers, wedding showers, birthdays...you name it, I love creating something unique and meaningful for the recipient.

Custom artwork can be anything from taking your own personal affirmations and putting them on paper in a beautiful design (as pictured above) to taking your favorite verse and creating beautiful artwork to hang on your wall.


While most of these pieces would be done on paper or stretched canvas, I can also offer custom designs to be printed on different clothing and household items. If this sounds like something you need, let's talk!


I love to work with you to create something meaningful and that fits your needs - both in budget, and design. Deliverables can vary as well including digital deliver, or physical delivery (either framed or not). I also offer flexibility on framing options. I have a professional framer that I use for projects, but that does come with increased production and shipping costs. - All things we will discuss before finalizing price and timelines.


From a favorite quote to wedding vows, if you have something you've wanted to have hand-lettered, let's talk! Send me an email at hello@mjhdesignstudio.com. I'd love to create something beautiful for you!
