My First Commission

You want to pay me for that?!?

8/21/2026 by Melissa Hundley



I've enjoyed hand lettering and calligraphy for a long time...but I can still remember when I thought to myself 'nobody would ever pay me to do that.'

I mean, some days I still tell myself that...but it's a lie, and I create beautiful pieces of art that lots of people would love to hang on their walls. My house is kind of my own personal art gallery...other than family pictures, there are very few things hanging on the walls that I didn't make.

So years ago when a friend asked me what I would charge to create a calligraphy piece for her...I had no idea what to say. I started thinking about what materials I would use, and how much they cost me and ended up with lots of random numbers that made almost no sense to me...let alone anyone else.

...so I gave her a number that would cover my costs...


She had given me the quote she wanted and mentioned she like yellow roses. Since the quote mentions rosebuds, I figured I needed to have yellow roses as part of the artwork. I should remind you...I'm a calligrapher...not a watercolor artist.

I poured over Pinterest and the rest of the internet looking for tutorials on how to paint simple but pretty roses. I managed to find a few and put bits and pieces of them together to come up with these very abstract roses that I ended up really loving (here's to trying new things!).

I practiced writing the quote so it would fit on the paper I had (and fit in a frame I could find at the craft store) and sent her a picture of it before I did the final version - just in case she didn't like it.


She loved it! So I completed the final draft, framed it and scheduled a time to deliver it to her. I was so happy to have created something for her that brought joy to her cute little home.


If you have a special quote or scripture passage that you would like turned into a beautiful calligraphy piece, please reach out! I'd love to create something beautiful for you! Send me an email at calligraphy@mjhdesignstudio.com
