Website Design Services

I can help design, build, and maintain your website


If you are in need of a custom website built for you and/or your business or organization, I am here to help!

I will work with you to define the feel and layout of your site as well as specific features to fit your needs.

I am a front-end developer with training from SheCodes. I work primarily in Visual Studio code, but have also worked on websites hosted on Wix and in WordPress, and am happy to work with whatever platform your currently have in place, or build you a new one.

Websites today should be responsive and adaptable to both desktop and mobile screens; I make it a priority to build websites that can adapt to both utilizing bootstrap and responsive design elements to optimize the experience for all users regardless of their device.


I prefer to work in HTML/CSS and Javascript, but can also work in React.js for more complicated sites and web applications.

If you're looking for recommendations on website hosting, design, or other website-related questions, please send me an email at developer@mjhdesignstudio.com
